Build Your Area's Band Plan

One table of where you may transmit and what each frequency is for near your station: the FCC rules for your license class, the ARRL band plan, your state frequency coordinator's band plan, and the local ARES, RACES and club plans. The FCC rules and the ARRL plan are built in and work with no internet; the rest you find once, then keep on this PC.

A · Once: what it needs

1
Your license class: Settings → Station agentWithout it the table shows the plans but not your privileges.
2
Where the station is: a grid square in Settings, or Nets → Find nets → locationOnly a rounded position (about 10 km) is ever sent.
3
The xAI key (Settings → Station agent): it searches and reads the plans

B · Find the local plans

4
Nets → Plans → set Within … km → Find local plansUp to two minutes; about $0.20–0.50. It looks for clubs and EMCOMM groups, your state coordinator's band plan, public ARES, RACES and ICS-205 plans, and the VHF/UHF Winlink gateways (in the band plan unless you untick them).
5
Choose the plans for your band plan: Plans and documents → tick In the band planThe ticked plan is saved on this PC if it is not yet (offline copy) and read into ranges and channels: one xAI call, a few cents. Untick to leave it out (ticking it again is free). Nothing is read until you tick it.
6
Winlink gateways, free: with Winlink Express on this PC, its channel list is read for VARA gateways (PUBLIC, EMCOMM) by distance from their grid squareRefresh it in Express: Channel Selection → Update Table Via Internet (the station asks when it is over 30 days old or never downloaded). VARA FM in range goes in; VARA HF is offered.
7
Members-only plan? Add a plan you have → choose the file (PDF, Word, text)The station never signs in anywhere. Your file stays on this PC and is read the same way.

C · Keep your channels

8
Frequencies found: open each source, tick the right ones → KeepNothing found is trusted until you keep it. "not opened" = the search did not read that page itself: check it.
9
A plan's regular net? Add its net puts it on the schedule
Kept channels name your radio's frequency on its card ("County ARES primary"), and Sidekick is told it. Plans change: when the last search is over a month old the Plans tab says so. Search again marks what is new and which kept channels a plan no longer lists. It never searches by itself.

Band plan → one table per bandShow: all, HF, VHF / UHF or one band · Only where I may transmit · Only what to check · Channels on or off · Print. Highest band first. Full spectrum: every stretch and channel with what is open there for ad hoc voice and VARA. Ad hoc voice / Ad hoc VARA: open ranges (HF), free simplex channels (VHF/UHF, 20 kHz steps, 25 on 70 cm), clear of every channel and gateway; green unplanned = no plan uses it. VARA FM: narrow only or narrow & wide (wide needs ~25 kHz). Use tunes a VARA radio there. 📻 marks what each radio is tuned to.

The columns

MHzA range (146.61–147.39), or a channel (shaded) with its mode, repeater offset and tone
UseWhat it is for, from the plan that speaks first (below), and whose words they are
YouRange: Phone · Data · CW, the ones struck through you may not use. Channel: yes / no
Sources · notesEvery other plan's words for it, and ⚠ anything to check

Who speaks first (how plans are reconciled)

FCC rulesThe law: decides the You column, never outranked
1 · State coordinatorThe area's band plan (repeater pairs, simplex, packet)
2 · Local plansARES, RACES, ICS-205, club plans and your own files
3 · Your channelsWhat you kept on the Plans tab
4 · ARRL band planThe national voluntary plan, built in

⚠ What to check

Plans differTwo plans set the same stretch aside for different uses (e.g. weak signal vs packet). Both are shown: follow the higher one, or ask the coordinator
Channel in another useA channel sits in a range set aside for something else (a simplex channel in repeater outputs)
Outside your privilegesA kept channel, or its repeater input, is outside your class
