VARA Chat and Files: Set Up and Connect

Keyboard chat and file transfer with another station over VARA HF or VARA FM, through the same VARA modem Winlink Express uses. Everything is sent in the clear and logged on this PC. Nothing transmits unless you click.

A · Once: set it up

1
A radio for VARA. On its card: Used for → Winlink onlyIts nets are not recorded on it, and the station never records from the VARA sound card.
2
Its Transmit switch on: Radios… → the radio → TransmitOff = VARA never transmits from this station.
3
Your license class: Settings → Station agentEvery transmission is checked against your data privileges on the radio's frequency.
4
The radio's frequency on its card (CAT reads it; or Change)
5
Open the VARA modem (VARA HF or VARA FM) as for Winlink, with its sound card and PTT set up there
6
The radio's card → VARA chat → choose VARA HF or VARA FM → Switch VARA onPorts 8300 / 8301 are VARA's defaults: change them only if you changed them in VARA. HF: Bandwidth 500, 2300 or 2750 Hz, or VARA's own setting.

B · Make a contact

7
Call a station: type its callsign → ConnectVARA FM can go through a digipeater: type it under via. A call you heard under CQs heard fills in with one tap.
8
Or call CQ: Call CQ → confirmListen first: the window shows channel busy when VARA hears the frequency in use.
9
Or wait for a call: tick Answer calls while I am hereIt turns itself off the moment you leave the PC or any check below fails.
When it will transmit, all four, every time: the radio is Winlink only · its Transmit switch is on · you are at the dashboard (keyboard or mouse in the last few minutes) · your license class has data privileges on that frequency. The window's green or amber line says which one is missing.

Chat, Files and Winlink Express

Share this card

Connected

1
Chat: type → EnterUp to 4000 characters a message. Theirs and yours show in the window with the time.
2
Send a file: File → choose itUp to 5 MB. A bar shows progress; the other station checks the file arrived whole (SHA-256) and says so. Small files: VARA is slow (HF, minutes for a page; FM is faster).
3
Files received are listed under the chat as downloadsSaved in data\vara\inbox, never opened by the station. Open one only if you expected it.
4
Finish: Disconnect · at once: AbortAbort drops the link immediately and lets go of the transmitter.

Who you can talk to

Another HAM Sidekick StationChat and files
Any other VARA keyboard programPlain chat, line by line ("plain terminal" in the window). Files need this program at both ends

Winlink Express (VARA serves one program at a time)

5
Hand VARA to Winlink Express → then open Express and connect as usual
6
Close Express → Take VARA backWhile Express is open the station leaves VARA alone.

When something looks wrong

"VARA is not answering"Open the VARA modem; check the ports match VARA's settings; close other programs using VARA
Connect is greyedRead the amber line: Transmit switch, you at the PC, license class, or the radio's frequency
"no data privileges"The frequency is outside your class's data segment: dashboard Band plan shows where you may transmit data
Stopped listeningYou left the PC, or a check failed: tick Answer calls again
