Where You May Go Ad Hoc

New here? Get the station

Band plan → Ad hoc VARA or Ad hoc voice lists, channel by channel, where you may start a contact that no plan has given to anything else. The FCC allows FM voice and data (VARA FM narrow and wide) on all of 2 m above 144.1 MHz, 1.25 m and 70 cm for a Technician and up (97.305): the band plans decide where.
